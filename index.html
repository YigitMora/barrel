LIPLINE

Support

Lipline is a single-player arcade game for iPhone. You ride a wave that is folding over behind you. The same wave gives you your speed, forms the face you ride, and closes on you if you get your line wrong — your accelerator and the thing that ends your run are the same object.

GETTING HELP

Questions, bug reports and feedback go here:

  • github.com/YigitMora/barrel/issues — open an issue. A GitHub account is required, and issues are public.

When reporting a problem, the most useful things to include are your device model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

WHAT THE APP DOES NOT DO

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics and no tracking.
  • No network access at all — the game works entirely offline.

YOUR DATA

Lipline collects nothing. Your best run, your progress and your two sound settings stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Hold to drive down the face of the wave and gain speed.
  • Let go to climb back up the face.
  • Stay in the pocket. Too high and the lip lands on you; too slow and the section closes ahead of you; too steep at speed and you bury the nose.
  • Time inside the barrel is what scores. Distance counts too.
  • Riding earns credit. Credit buys boards, and access to bigger and more dangerous waves.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored.
